## Armas Especializadas

### Active Denial System (ADS)

Desenvolvimento novo em crowd control. Vehicle mounted; uso limitado outdoor operations e perimeter security (driving escaping blankbodies back building, away civilians, por exemplo). ADS emits pinpoint accurate intense heat ray, até second-degree burns. Second Inquisition turns heat up.

**Efeito:** nenhum damage até 3o turno consecutive exposure; depois **1 dano Superficial each turn**. Qualquer pessoa (including blankbodies) em area of effect faz **Resolve + Composure roll** avoid backing/running away; Difficulty aumenta 1 each turn. ADS amped = **Aggravated damage**.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1264–1267.*

### Compound Crossbow

Efficient time-honored stake delivery. Cables increase bow power. 200 lbs. draw hits hard: shatter blankbody sternum easily. Called shot heart não mais fácil, raw power = pulverize organ hit.

**Dano:** +2.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1268–1270.*

### Flare Gun

Pistol-shaped ou rifle-attached launcher. Standard strontium/potassium flares. Calcium flares ready underwater (flooded crypts, sewers). Legal, easy obtain; UTR carry distress signals, sometimes weapons. Farm Teams/Amateurs pode primary weapon.

**Dano:** 2 Aggravated. Enclosed space: +1 Difficulty **Composure e Terror Frenzy tests** 1 turno.

**Variante - Road Flare:** street-legal incendiaries. Light, throw **Strength + Athletics** target. **1 dano Aggravated**. Not thrown immediately = wielder takes **1 Aggravated** molten residue drips hand.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1271–1275.*

### Garrote

Military-issue: piano wire length between wooden handles. Decapitate smooth motion, effective blankbodies/mortals equally.

**Called shot behead penalty:** reduzido 1 dado. **Damage bonus:** +0. Sneak-up win (Dexterity + Stealth vs. Wits + Awareness contest) = adiciona margin desse roll + attack roll margin to damage calculation.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1276–1278.*

### Long Range Acoustic Device (LRAD)

"Sonic cannon" crowd control. 300 lbs.; vehicle mounted, outdoor spaces. Induces headaches, panic, hearing impairment; funciona vampires.

**Efeito:** anyone firing arc faz **Stamina + Composure** (Difficulty 4) resist **3 dano Superficial each round**. Failed = panicked (**-2 dice Composure e Intelligence tests**) som lasts. Vampiros panicked 3 consecutive rounds fazem **Frenzy test**.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1279–1282.*

### Nightwatchman (ESOG)

Shortsword disguised regular baton. Carry without drawing attention, unsheath moment, regular sword use. Treasured possession—each through hell/high water owner. Some marked body counts, scripture lines, saint names/loved ones.

Otimizado beheading: short straight blade cuts clean.

**Called shot decapitate penalty:** -1. **Dano:** +2.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1283–1285.*

### Riot Foam

Slows truck certainly hinders blankbody, até one unnatural speed. Unless strong truck too. Coating target gasoline increases subsequent fire damage +2. Miracle holy water tank: treat sunlight damage.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1286–1292.*

## Munição

Standard Dragon's Breath e Raufoss incendiary (Core p. 380) mais varied loads:

### Armor-Piercing Rounds

Harder metal, less liable deform impact. Keep shape, rip through body armor instead compress/stop. Trivial blankbodies, excellent neutralizing armored mortal/ghoul bodyguards (common vampire retinue part).

**Efeito:** reduz target's Armor Value 2.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1296–1298.*

### Breaching Rounds

Shotgun shell "master key" designed break impact. Blows lock/hinges without hurting behind. Fired human inflicts chunk damage. Standard shotgun does much same door damage; UTR mostly breaching rounds extractions: retrieving team members/informants prisoner blankbodies, important controlling harm.

**Efeito:** shotgun loaded behaves standard ammo, mas no chance hitting multiple targets/ricocheting.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1299–1301.*

### Flash-Bang Grenades

Stun grenade produces momentary blinding light and deaf blast confusing seconds. UTR disorient targets raid start, cover escape.

**Mechanic:** Dexterity + Athletics throw, contest Wits + Resolve. Winner loses only disoriented (**-2 dice Wits pools**) 1 round. Otherwise unable see. Eye protection **+2 dice Wits + Resolve roll**.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1302–1304.*

### Plastic Explosives

Semtex/C4 advantage: set advance, detonate distance (detonation cord/timer). TTrig spark detonates. UTR breaching buildings breaching rounds won't, ou traps. Alternatively blankbody too dangerous take, few C4 bricks complete destruction building over Haven might do.

**Setting:** Intelligence + Technology roll (Core p. 410). Rough usage: 200g destroys car, **7 níveis dano Aggravated**. +3 níveis each additional 200g; blast area increase accordingly.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1305–1307.*

### Rubber Bullets

"Less lethal" projectiles serious bruising, bone break, blind target. Sometimes UTR wants ghoul/mortal retainer custody; close useless blankbodies.

**Efeito:** firearm loaded deals **Superficial damage** mortals e vampires alike.

"Wooden bullets" crowd control stubby discs shotgun casing likewise no Aggravated anyone. Stake shotgun barrel (replace shot wad) penetrates vampire sternum point-blank range like stake-launcher (Core p. 381).

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1308–1311.*

### Thermite

Phosphorus/metal oxide mix burns 2,200° C. Through steel/asphalt, flesh from bone. Prolonged bone ash. SI nickname "instant crematorium."

Obvious breaching uses: sticky explosive planted, ignited, left detonate. Specially constructed grenades (magnesium fuse spark). Once ignited, extremely challenging extinguish. Smothering ineffective (oxygen inside thermite), submersion water ineffective. Burns upwards 30 seconds.

Relatively easy make (Intelligence + Science roll, Difficulty 3); buy small quantities welding supply.

**Efeito:** **3 dano Aggravated each round, 3 rounds.**

Introducing thermite sleeping blankbody every Inquisitor dream, every hunter fantastic method own. Mixing Tzimisce's jar dirt, por exemplo...

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1312–1317.*

### Thermobaric Munitions

Mostly rifle grenade UTR loadouts, slower more intense explosions than standard. Efficient room-clearing, advantage setting everyone inside fire. Forcing entry: major structure damage. "Bunker busters."

**Efeito:** **+5 dano Aggravated** (vs. +4 regular grenades), set hit fire. **+3 dados** rolls damage structures.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1318–1320.*

### White Phosphorus

Pyrophoric: ignites air contact. Some incendiary grenades, primarily smoke grenades. Like flash-bangs, UTR squads cover arrival/departure, provide advantage blankbodies mind-altering abilities.

**Efeito:** reduz vision-reliant dice pools **3 dados**. Dominate eye-contact inefetivo; **+2 dice** resist Presence effects.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1321–1323.*

## Armor

Ephesians 6:11, St. Paul advises "put on whole armor of God, stand against devil's wiles." Coalition suggests adding other field armor.

### Chainmail

Menos effective Kevlar mas makes statement. Traditional steel says "care far more aesthetics protection" modern titanium alloys advanced materials uses. Society St. Leopold particularly enamored: "holy crusader" full suit.

**Steel chainmail:** Armor Value 3 (1 vs. bullets), **-2 dice Stealth, -1 dado other Dexterity tests**. **Modern chainmail:** Armor Value 3 (including bullets), no Dexterity penalty. Usually covered textile outer muffling sound (negating Stealth penalty); some agents—mostly Leopoldites—opt grand gesture visible.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1326–1328.*

### Kevlar Vest

Kevlar flak jackets/body armor substantial protection **(Armor Value 4)** cost flexibility/speed **(-1 dado Dexterity tests)**. Small clumsiness price surviving op; most UTR standard field loadout.

Flame-resistant (Armor Value 1 fire), default choice Clearance Specialists flamethrowers, anyone alongside.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1329–1331.*

### Neck Protector

Not especially protective, discreet. Motorcycle protector hidden bulky coat, carbon fiber fits scarf/high collar. Popular hunters without better gear (Amateurs, Farm Teams).

**Efeito:** motorcycle/carbon fiber protectors—**Armor Value 1 (0 bullets)**. All neck protectors **+2 dice Strength + Brawl** resist bite attack.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1332–1334.*

### Riot Shield

Lots advantages: easy put down if agent needs pass civilian. Defensive weapon: shove assailant force one. Clear polycarbonate não impede vision.

**Police riot shields:** Armor Value 3 (0 surprised behind), 0 ballistics. SAD/BOES variant somewhat bulletproof: **Armor Value 1 ballistics**.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1335–1337.*

## Equipamento Mundano

Stuff lying around safehouse. All organized Five Torches teams access; Amateurs need Resources.

### Battering Ram

Police issue applies 3 metric tonnes force door locks, one average strength person operate. Lockpick guns quieter, ram makes entrance. Teams want make stir use.

UK police favorite, SO13 know Enforcer.

**Efeito:** **+3 dice** any test force open door.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1340–1343.*

### Dental Pliers

Blankbody dragged interrogation, fang rippers come out. Sensible risk management. Excellent threat: expect survive, dignity/pride forever ruined.

**Mecânica:** removing fang successful **Strength + Medicine or Brawl** vs. restrained target. Personnel like fill extraction site molten metal/bone putty, belief stops regeneration.

**Efeito:** fang remove = **1 dano Aggravated**. All fangs + gap fill = Baby Teeth Flaw (Core p. 182).

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1344–1348.*

### Fiberoptic Scope

Flexible optical fiber bundle, lens end, eyepiece/camera attach. Peeking inside small spaces: locks, machines, inside body. UTR mostly first two: aid lockpicking, understanding/disarming traps security, subtle surveillance inserting under doors/around frames.

**Efeito:** **+2 dados** tests these purposes.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1349–1351.*

### GPS Trackers

Coalition keep track surveillance targets, keep tabs personnel, especially Cuckoos/high-risk. Vehicle trackers usually magnetic, smaller palm-sized. Spotting requires **Wits + Streetwise ou Technology** success; removing simple. Personnel trackers watch-face size, sewn clothing, worn shoes, stuck watch underside. Finding **Difficulty 3 Wits + Streetwise/Technology**.

**Efeito:** active tracker **+4 dados** any test locate wearer.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1352–1355.*

### Ground Penetrating Radar

Uses radar pulses image subsurface features. Penetrates metres dirt, solid concrete/rock. Finds deepest Nosferatu sewer/Tzimisce crypt, beyond warded Tremere chantry stone walls. Spots hidden room/bunker, traces power lines/water pipes leading.

Operation easy subtle: lawnmower-sized devices easily mistaken surveying/utilities workers.

**Efeito:** operation requires **Difficulty 2 Intelligence + Technology** roll.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1356–1358.*

### Laser Microphone

Small easily-hidden microphone uses laser beam "read" sound vibrations. Tiny discreet, Coalition go-to audio surveillance. Needs attach flat vibrating surface—window/painting. Windows ideal: mic doesn't even need inside room.

Pickup range limited: exceptionally large room can stymie. Hard find (**Wits + Technology roll, Difficulty 5**).

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1359–1361.*

### Lockpick Gun

Lockpicking requires delicate hand, extensive training. Or device fires steel rod lock, releases cylinder, sticks fine metal rod open. Much faster than normal, matter seconds. Also quiet: soft punch/click.

*Fonte: Second Inquisition, cap. 2, txt:1362–1363.*

## XTechnology (Devices Especiais)

XTechnology são gadgets experimentais contra Disciplines e poderes vampíricos específicos. Frequentemente desenvolvidos XTechnician antagonistas ou fabricados laboratoricamente. Exemplos incluem:

- **Screamer grenades:** contra Heightened Senses (Auspex)
- **Tanglefoot traps:** contra Celerity
- **Magnetic fields:** contra Blood Sorcery
- **Specialized detection systems:** contra Obfuscate
